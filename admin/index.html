How to read this page
decide
Things only you can call, most urgent first. Each links to where you act on it.
red / amber / green
Per active project. Red: creep at or above 70%, or blocked. Amber: creep at or above 50%, time share 10+ points off target, or a review date within 14 days. Otherwise green. The reason prints under the dot. Parked projects show grey unless their review is near.
goals · major / minor
What each project is for. Major = the point of the project; minor = worth doing, not the point. A prompt serves a goal when it contains one of that goal's keywords.
on-goal
Share of your typed prompts on a project that served a goal, split into major and minor. A prompt that serves both counts as major.
creep
Of your asks for new capability, the share that served no goal. High creep means building things you didn't set out to build.
share / target
Your hours on a project as a share of all project hours, against the share you intended.
wall-clock vs session hours
Wall-clock counts overlapping sessions once (real time spent); session hours add them up, so they run higher.
API-equivalent
What your Claude usage would cost at API prices. Not a bill; real costs and revenue are under money.
revenue · costs · net
Entered by hand under money, per project per month (net = revenue − costs). The portfolio's budget column shows this month's net. "≈ $/h" divides this month's net by the last 28 days' hours — a rough guide, the windows don't line up.
▲ ▼
Change against a week ago. Colour says good or bad: creep going up is red, on-goal going up is green. "–" means no change or no history yet.
freshness
Computed on the Mac every 3h; the header turns amber when a snapshot is over a day old. The page is read-only except goals.
